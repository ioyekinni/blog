# Why We Invested in ẸyọOwó

**IOY Ventures | Pre-Seed Announcement**

---

After months of disciplined deal flow and rigorous due diligence (a 45-second demo on FaceTime), we are thrilled to announce that IOY Ventures has led a **Z$31,099,211,400 pre-seed round** into **ẸyọOwó**, the household finance platform we believe will define the next generation of consumer fintech.

We don't say this lightly. In fact, we almost passed. The round was competitive — Sage had already received a verbal commitment of "that's cool" from at least two other friends — and our partnership had to move fast. In venture, speed is a signal.

## The Problem

Every year, households lose thousands of dollars to financial disorganization. The existing tools — Mint (rest in peace), YNAB, spreadsheets taped to refrigerators — were built for individuals, not for the messy, real-world dynamics of shared household money. Who owes whom? Where is the money actually going? Why is there a $47 DoorDash charge at 1 AM? These are trillion-dollar questions.

## The Product

ẸyọOwó is a full-stack household finance platform featuring multi-person tracking, category-level expense analysis, a built-in debt ledger, recurring entry management, and intelligent distribution modeling. These are features that most consumer budgeting apps charge a subscription for and still get wrong.

What struck us immediately was the product's **authenticity**. This was not built in a Stanford dorm room by someone who read a Paul Graham essay about ramen profitability. This was built by a founder who needed to solve a real problem in his own household, in production, under real domestic conditions. The app has been stress-tested against the most demanding QA environment in consumer software: **daily use by a real couple managing real money.**

That kind of organic product-market fit cannot be manufactured by a growth team.

## The Founder

Sage is a classic builder-founder — resourceful, velocity-oriented, and pathologically committed to shipping. He took the product from concept to functional PWA using conversational AI as a force multiplier, a workflow we internally refer to as **"vibe infrastructure."** Some investors see this as unconventional. We see it as capital efficiency taken to its logical extreme.

Most founding teams burn $2M before achieving product-market fit. Sage achieved it over dinner.

We were also impressed by his architectural instincts. Firebase backend. PWA-first distribution. Zero App Store dependency. These are the kinds of decisions a seasoned CTO makes after three failed startups. Sage made them on his first build.

## The Market

The global personal finance software market is valued at over $1.1B and growing. ẸyọOwó is positioned to capture an underserved segment we internally call **"couples who are tired of arguing about subscriptions."** Our models suggest this TAM is functionally infinite.

The product's current retention metrics are staggering: **100% DAU, 0% churn.** The sample size is small, but the conviction is absolute.

## Deal Terms

IOY Ventures deployed Z$31,099,211,400 at a valuation we are keeping confidential, which we arrived at by multiplying the number of Firebase read operations by the current price of jollof rice in Lagos and adding a vibes premium. For the uninitiated, that is approximately **thirty-one billion 2008 Zimbabwean dollars** — a figure that, on paper, makes this one of the largest pre-seed rounds in the history of vibe-coded fintech.

IOY Ventures will hold a board observer seat, which in practice means we will text Sage unsolicited product feedback approximately twice a month.

## What We're Excited About

- **PWA-first distribution.** No App Store tax. No Tim Cook oversight. No 30% revenue haircut. Just clean, direct-to-consumer distribution via the browser — the way the internet was supposed to work.
- **Naming moat.** Try Googling "ẸyọOwó." There is zero competition for this keyword. In an era where every fintech name is either a misspelled English word or two random syllables followed by ".io," ẸyọOwó is linguistically irreducible. We consider this a durable competitive advantage.
- **Genuine product depth.** Category tracking, debt ledger, recurring entries, distribution logic — this is not a wrapper around a spreadsheet. The feature set reflects real iteration born from real usage.
- **Clear commercialization path.** The roadmap from private tool to public product is well-defined: multi-tenant auth, payment integration via Lemon Squeezy, onboarding flows, and a landing page at eyoowo.app. Most pre-seed companies are still arguing about their color palette. ẸyọOwó has a launch plan.

## Our Thesis

At IOY Ventures, we back founders who build for themselves first and discover the business model through lived experience. Sage did not set out to build a fintech company. He set out to bring clarity to his household's finances. That is the kind of authentic problem-solving that pitch decks cannot manufacture and accelerator programs cannot teach.

We believe ẸyọOwó has the DNA of a product people will genuinely love — because one person already built it out of genuine need, and it works.
