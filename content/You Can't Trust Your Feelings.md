
Larin, an old friend, used to say this a lot: "*Your feelings aren't real.*"
I didn't think much of it then, but more and more these days, I see what she meant.
If you just follow your feelings, you'll rack up a lot of mistakes in your life.

It's like how we have rules in society - there are things you don't just do irrespective of how you feel;  *You don't just say some things;* *You don't beat up people just cos you feel like it, even though you might feel justified;* *You don't just take things just because you find them desirable, or take another person's possession just because you want it.

There are established societal rules that we must follow, irrespective of our feelings. These rules separate us from beasts, and it is thanks to them that we are not savages.
With them, we aspire to our best, and without them, we degenerate to the worst of our nature.
