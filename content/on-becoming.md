# On Becoming

I recently listened to an interview with Angie You and Richard Heyman, and one idea stuck with me: *genius capacity*.

Some things you just pick up faster than everyone else. You learn them quickly, the interest is obvious, and that energy is contagious — it's what draws in both teammates and backers.

Discipline matters, but it compounds differently depending on where you apply it. You can be disciplined about anything, but the ROI varies wildly. Layer discipline on top of something you already have genius capacity for — something you're already deeply passionate about — and the returns become outsized. That's the multiplier. That's how you build an asymmetric life.
