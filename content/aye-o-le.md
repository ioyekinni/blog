# Ayé Ò Le

*August 24, 2023*

*"Bóhùn kópẹ́ títí, ayé wa ń bọ̀ wá dópin"*
— Infinity

Though morbid, there's something very liberating about knowing death will surely come. No matter what we do, no matter what we achieve, whether we experience pain & misery or have pleasurable experiences, peace, and joy, no matter the memories and experiences that define our lives, sooner or later, it will surely come to an end. At first glance, it appears dark & morbid, or nihilistic, but in realizing this brutal truth of life, it is surprisingly liberating. This truth says, live your life. Don't take it too seriously, and don't get hung up on anything; sooner or later, it'll end. Live life while you're alive – eat, sleep, drink, dance, make love, learn, and enjoy the pleasures of the living – they who are no more cannot enjoy these pleasures.
